== STANDARD PROPOSAL STRUCTURE ==

Section 1 — Executive Summary (1 page)
- Client pain points addressed
- Our recommended solution in 3 sentences
- Key business outcomes (quantified)
- Our differentiators

Section 2 — Requirements Understanding
- Restate client requirements from RFP
- Identify gaps or clarifications needed
- Map requirements to SAP modules

Section 3 — Proposed Solution
- Solution architecture
- SAP modules and version
- Deployment approach (cloud/hybrid/on-premise)
- Integration landscape

Section 4 — Case Studies (2–3 most relevant)
- Same industry or same module as client
- Quantified results only

Section 5 — Proposed Team
- Project Manager profile
- Functional leads (1 per module)
- Technical lead
- Average experience: 7.2 years SAP

Section 6 — Timeline & Phasing
- Phase 1: Blueprinting (weeks 1–8)
- Phase 2: Build & Configure (weeks 9–24)
- Phase 3: Testing — SIT/UAT (weeks 25–36)
- Phase 4: Go-Live & Hypercare (weeks 37–46)

Section 7 — Investment Summary
- Fixed-price breakdown by phase
- Assumptions and exclusions
- Hypercare included (8 weeks standard)
