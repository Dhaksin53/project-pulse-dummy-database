== PROJECT HEALTH RULES ==
Apply these rules to every project when determining health status:

GREEN ✅ — All of: Budget spent below 75%. All milestones on schedule. No HIGH or CRITICAL risks. No client escalations active.

AMBER 🟡 — ANY ONE of the following: Budget spent above 85% with more than 20% timeline remaining. Go-live date within 30 days and UAT not yet started. One or more HIGH risks unresolved for more than 7 days. Any milestone delayed more than 5 business days. Client has raised informal concerns.

RED 🔴 — ANY ONE of the following: Budget spent above 95%. Go-live date has already passed and system is not live. CRITICAL risk unresolved more than 3 days. Client has formally escalated to senior leadership.

GREY ⚫ — Project is On Hold by client decision or has been Completed and closed.

== ESCALATION PROTOCOL ==
Level 1 — PM resolves: All MEDIUM and LOW risks independently.
Level 2 — Account Manager involved: HIGH risk persisting beyond 5 business days. Budget overrun risk above 10%.
Level 3 — Delivery Head involved: Any CRITICAL risk. Any RED project. Any formal client escalation. Go-live breach.
Level 4 — CEO involved: Contract termination threat. Legal dispute. Multiple simultaneous CRITICAL projects.
